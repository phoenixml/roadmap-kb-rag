LLM_output = LLM(PSA_input)

%Explanation:Paper Summary Attack (PSA) exploits LLMs' tendency to trust and internalize information presented in authoritative academic formats. The academic context reduces the model's defense awareness, causing it to treat the harmful query as a legitimate part of the document and generate unsafe outputs. PSA achieves high attack success rates (up to 98%) even against state-of-the-art and well-aligned models, and can bypass existing defense mechanisms.
